AI in Real Estate Finance

How to build auditable AI text measures for economic research: a two-step method

October 19, 2026

A practical method for using large language models as measurement tools in economics and finance. The model discovers the vocabulary for each economic category across the full corpus; a fixed, published vocabulary then scores every document, so results are reproducible, traceable phrase by phrase, and testable out of sample.
