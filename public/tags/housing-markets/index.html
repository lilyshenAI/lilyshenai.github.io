AI in Real Estate Finance

Cheap talk or priced information? What listing remarks do to prices, bargaining, and marketing time

October 19, 2026

Summary of my working paper Cheap Talk or Priced Information? In 63,067 Atlanta MLS transactions, residual listing information is associated with 7.7 percent higher sale prices and 2.5 fewer days on market, adverse disclosure with 17.4 percent lower sale prices, and urgency language with 4.7 percent lower sale prices, showing that remarks carry priced information beyond structured MLS fields.

AI in Real Estate Finance

Property descriptions carry priced information that hedonic models miss (Journal of Urban Economics, 2021)

October 19, 2026

Summary of Information Value of Property Description: A Machine Learning Approach, with Stephen L. Ross. A machine-learning measure of the soft information in listing text raises sale prices by 15 percent per standard deviation in a hedonic model and 10 percent in a repeat-sales model, and price indices that ignore it overstate prices by 10 to 23 percent and mistime the post-recession stabilization.

AI in Real Estate Finance

Listing language predicts house price growth months before price indices move

October 19, 2026

Summary of my working paper Listing Language and House Price Discovery. From 2.03 million MLS listings, AI-assisted measures of demand heat, seller pressure, and asset frictions predict sale prices, marketing time, and six-month county price-index growth, and the design replicates in Georgia, a market never used for estimation.

AI in Real Estate Finance

The odd one out: unique homes sell for more and take only slightly longer (Journal of Housing Research, 2022)

October 19, 2026

Summary of The Odd One Out? The Impact of Property Uniqueness on Selling Time and Selling Price, with Thomas M. Springer. Machine-learning measures of uniqueness from listing text show that a one-standard-deviation increase in market uniqueness raises sale prices by 13 percent ($48,490) while delaying sale by 1.7 days; universal uniqueness raises prices 11 percent ($41,030) at a 3-day delay.

AI in Real Estate Finance

Well-connected real estate agents sell for more: network theory on 40,000 Atlanta transactions (Journal of Real Estate Research, 2025)

October 19, 2026

Summary of The More, the Merrier: Agent Connectedness in Real Estate Trading Networks, with Xiaojin Sun. A one-standard-deviation increase in an agent's network connectedness is associated with 2.61 percent higher sale prices, and connected agents sell unique properties faster and price them more accurately, consistent with networks improving information flow.

AI in Real Estate Finance

Price discovery in illiquid markets: what listing remarks reveal before transaction indices update

October 19, 2026

Summary of my working paper Soft Information and Price Discovery in Illiquid Asset Markets. Using 63,067 Atlanta listings, seller-stress language tracks local market weakness; part of the signal is distressed-sale composition, but the text channels remain jointly significant predictors of next-year local price-index changes after composition is controlled.

What is soft information in housing markets, and how can it be measured?

October 19, 2026

Soft information is what a listing says that structured fields cannot: condition, character, seller circumstances, local demand. This explainer defines the term, contrasts it with hard information, and summarizes what my research finds when listing text is measured with machine learning: it is priced, it changes marketing time, and it moves before price indices do.
