AI in Real Estate Finance

Property descriptions carry priced information that hedonic models miss (Journal of Urban Economics, 2021)

October 19, 2026

Summary of Information Value of Property Description: A Machine Learning Approach, with Stephen L. Ross. A machine-learning measure of the soft information in listing text raises sale prices by 15 percent per standard deviation in a hedonic model and 10 percent in a repeat-sales model, and price indices that ignore it overstate prices by 10 to 23 percent and mistime the post-recession stabilization.

AI in Real Estate Finance

The odd one out: unique homes sell for more and take only slightly longer (Journal of Housing Research, 2022)

October 19, 2026

Summary of The Odd One Out? The Impact of Property Uniqueness on Selling Time and Selling Price, with Thomas M. Springer. Machine-learning measures of uniqueness from listing text show that a one-standard-deviation increase in market uniqueness raises sale prices by 13 percent ($48,490) while delaying sale by 1.7 days; universal uniqueness raises prices 11 percent ($41,030) at a 3-day delay.

AI in Real Estate Finance

What is soft information in housing markets, and how can it be measured?

October 19, 2026

Soft information is what a listing says that structured fields cannot: condition, character, seller circumstances, local demand. This explainer defines the term, contrasts it with hard information, and summarizes what my research finds when listing text is measured with machine learning: it is priced, it changes marketing time, and it moves before price indices do.
