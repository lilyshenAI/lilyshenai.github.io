Teaching and textbooks

I have taught real estate at Clemson since 2016, in person and online, and started the university's first hands-on AI courses in finance in 2018. I coauthor two of the standard textbooks in the field and write the test banks for both.

Textbooks

Commercial Real Estate Analysis for Investment, Finance and Development, 4th edition

David M. Geltner, Norman G. Miller, Alex van de Minne, Piet Eichholtz, Thies Lindenthal, Lily Shen · Routledge, 2026

The standard advanced text in commercial real estate. Lily Shen wrote the material on AI and proptech and the test bank; free companion resources at CREBook.net and the podcast CRE Explained.

Companion site

Real Estate Finance and Investments, 2027 edition

Jeffrey D. Fisher, Jacob Sagi, Stephanie R. Yates, Lily Shen · McGraw Hill, 2027

The widely adopted foundational text in real estate finance, developed by William Brueggeman and Jeffrey Fisher. Lily Shen wrote the new chapter on AI in real estate finance and the test bank.

Free companion resources: CREBook.net (slides, spreadsheets, exercises) and the podcast CRE Explained.

How I teach

  • More than 1,700 students taught since 2016; Spring 2026 Real Estate Finance sections above university, college, and department benchmarks on every evaluation item.
  • Clemson College of Business Dean's Award for Student Engagement, 2019–20 (single annual recipient).
  • Founded Clemson's undergraduate real estate speaker series (2021): bank executives, brokerage CEOs, and Federal Reserve economists in the classroom.
  • Former faculty adviser, Clemson Real Estate Club; mentees have gone to Amazon, Deloitte, Truist, and Cisco.
  • Program building: recruiting and curriculum committees for Clemson's M.S. in Finance; proposals for a real estate study-abroad program in London and Cambridge, a university real estate center, and a signature experiential-learning program in real estate.
  • Host of CRE Explained, the companion podcast to Commercial Real Estate Analysis; free teaching materials at CREBook.net.
