Associate Professor of Finance, Clemson University · Visiting Scholar, Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta

Associate Professor of Finance at Clemson University and visiting scholar at the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta. I study housing and mortgage markets and use machine learning and large language models to turn text into auditable economic measures. Coauthor of two standard real estate textbooks.

I have worked on real estate since my undergraduate years, and I still like it for the same reason: it is where finance meets actual places and the people who live and work in them.
