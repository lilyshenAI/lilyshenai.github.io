Textbooks

I have taught real estate at Clemson since 2016, in person and online, and started the university's first hands-on AI courses in finance in 2018. I coauthor two of the standard textbooks in the field and wrote the assessment materials for both books in their entirety. For Commercial Real Estate Analysis I was responsible for the chapters on mortgages and the secondary mortgage market, and for Real Estate Finance and Investments I wrote the new chapter on artificial intelligence in real estate finance and investment.
