Your call

NOI $89,910. Asking price $1,380,000. What do you do?

There is no single right answer. What matters is the NOI and the cap rate behind your number.

  1. NOI is what a building earns from operations: rent and miscellaneous income collected, minus operating expenses and capex.
  2. Capex goes into NOI a little every year, not the whole bill at once.
  3. Value = NOI ÷ cap rate.
